Team
Myself, 3 other Product Designers, 2 Service Designers, 2 LLM Lead Architects, 1 Developer

Background

As an employee with a busy schedule I need issues identified and resolved automatically / quickly so that it does not impact my work.

User stories

  • As an employee who is part of a collaborative team I need a mechanism to flag to my team when I am experiencing tech issues
  • As an employee I need to be able to identify tech-related issues as soon as they occur so that I do not get stuck in the middle of my work
  • As an employee with a busy schedule I need issues identified and resolved quickly so that it does not impact my work

User research

We surveyed 18 people within the firm, who shared with us how they find the level of complexity of resolving tech issues internally. Tech issues are tedious and drag on for an extended period of time.

90%

of JPMC respondents have faced tech issues preventing them from doing their work

67%

tech issues may last more than 2 hours

34%

find it hard / very hard to resolve the tech issues

28% of respondents take longer than 2 days and up to a week to solve their issues.

Painpoints and drivers

Current tech hub chatbot relies on agent resolution and can only be accessed through the website.

What can APEX AI do?

  • Identification — Identify when a user is experiencing a problem
  • Learning — Assistant learns from various internal data sources and previous user queries
  • Resolution — Easy fixes are automated; the assistant suggests fixes to user queries and can raise a ticket for the user, online or offline

Outcome

The concept placed 6th of 32 teams in 48 hours. The survey behind it — 18 colleagues, 90% of whom had lost working time to a tech issue and 28% of whom waited days for a fix — turned an anecdotal complaint into a quantified case for automating first-line resolution instead of routing every issue to an agent.
