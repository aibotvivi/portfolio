Askme.bot

What happens to your questions

Askme.bot is an automated assistant that answers in my voice from what I have written. I keep some of the questions people ask it, so I can see what it gets wrong and fill the gaps.

What is kept

The question as typed or tapped, which door you chose, which topic it matched, how it was answered, the time, and a random code for that one conversation so its questions can be read in order. No name, account, cookie or IP address is stored with it. Email addresses and phone numbers are blanked before a question is saved, both in your browser and again in the database.

When

“I’m hiring”: questions are saved, as the note under the chat says. I rely on a legitimate interest in improving the bot. If you would rather they were not kept, email me instead of using the chat.

“Tell me about your healing practice”: nothing is saved unless you tick the box under the chat. Questions there can touch on health, which deserves your explicit say-so. Untick it at any time and later questions are not kept.

How long, and who sees it

Ninety days, then each question is deleted automatically. Only I can read them. They are stored with Supabase, the database service this site already uses for its sign-up list, and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

If the chat’s answers are being written by Claude, your question is also sent to Anthropic to write the reply; the note under the chat says when that is the case.

Your say

To have your questions deleted sooner, or to ask anything about this, email vivienchinux@gmail.com with roughly when you used the chat and what you asked. Vivien Chin, London, is responsible for this data. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
