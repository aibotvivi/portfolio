Side project

Postmarked

Travel map and digital postcard studio — an interactive world map of city suggestions, plus a postcard maker with stamps, typewriter captions and postmarks.

Stack
Vite, React, TypeScript, Leaflet
Runs at
npm run dev → localhost:5173
Status
MVP

Background

Two halves of one idea: a world map of travel suggestions by city, and a postcard studio that turns a photo into something you would actually send.

How it works

  • Paper reference — the postcard follows a real one: photo print top-left, perforated stamp with a globe cancellation and black ring postmark top-right, typewriter caption below, a dashed divider and a 📍 line at the foot.
  • Exports a PNG — the finished card downloads as an image.
  • No backend — state is shared entirely through a base64url link.
