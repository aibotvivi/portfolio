Background

Tibetan bowl sessions aimed at a specific nervous system rather than at "relaxation" in general. The product is three named sessions — Focus Flow (ADHD, 25 min), Regulate (anxiety and attachment, 10 min) and Wind Down (sleep, 20 min) — sold as a membership at £72 a year, with a £250 tier for work made to your intention.

How it works

  • The app is deliberately not built yet — the sequence is waitlist, then payment links, then fulfilling the first members by hand, and only then application code. Three kill criteria decide whether that last phase happens at all.
  • Named sessions, not a library — three sessions with a stated purpose each, rather than a content catalogue. No streaks, no badges, no app-blocking, no social feed; the product is meant to be closed, not returned to.
  • Two tiers with a real difference — Core is the three sessions on your own schedule; Circle adds a monthly track composed for your intention, a live group session and a quarterly 梅花易數 reading paired with a custom track.
