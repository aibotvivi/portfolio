Background

A reading app covering tarot, I-Ching and angel numbers, in Traditional Chinese with an English toggle. The front end is a single static page; a Node server handles readings.

How it works

  • Retrieval, not invention — reference knowledge for each drawn card or hexagram is injected into the prompt from a local dataset, so readings stay anchored to the source imagery rather than being improvised.
  • Feedback loop — every reading is logged, and 👍-rated ones become few-shot examples for later prompts.
  • Three modes — tarot spreads, I-Ching hexagrams and angel numbers share one interface.
