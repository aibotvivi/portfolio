Side project

Anti-Flare

Eczema anti-inflammatory diet app — a daily fact, habit tracking, and a camera scan that reads a meal and rates its likely impact.

Stack
Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind v4, Claude Vision
Runs at
npm run dev
Data
localStorage only — no account

Background

A companion for managing eczema through diet: what to eat, what to avoid, and whether yesterday actually helped. Everything stays on the device — there is no sign-up and no server-side record.

How it works

  • Point the camera at a meal — the photo goes to a vision model that returns a structured assessment of its likely eczema impact rather than a calorie count.
  • Evidence on the card — daily facts cite their source, so the advice can be checked rather than taken on trust.
  • Habits, gently — eight daily habits tracked without streak pressure.
