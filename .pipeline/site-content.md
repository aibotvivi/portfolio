## Case studies (4)

### 1. In-Car Payments (Mobility Payments Solutions)
- Card title on homepage: "Mobile App & In-Car Infotainment" — "Researched and developed a design system utilizing Google Automotive OS" for CES 2024/2025
- Role: Senior Product Designer · Timeline: Q3 2022 – present
- Problem: white-label wallet for seamless, secure payments from automotive OS — EV charging, parking, QSR, device binding
- Process: competitive analysis, cross-functional workshops (product/legal/dev), mobility-scenario user research (short trips, commuting, road trips), prototyping on Google Automotive OS with Samsung S9 tablet demos, iterating within Automotive OS + design-system constraints
- Outcome: secured key technical partnerships in 2023; pursuing first pilot client + solution integration for 2024
- Target users: drivers, passengers; demos for OEM brand partners

### 2. Livestreaming via Qurio App (QVC) — Presenter, Viewer and Moderator Flow
- Role: UX/UI Designer and Researcher · Timeline: 2 months (8 sprints)
- Problem: aging user base (40–60) on smaller phones; reduce cognitive load while balancing button sizes and feature prioritisation for viewing/questioning/interaction
- Process: host interviews (livestream setup, feature navigation, comment readability); validation with 5 potential viewers (badge comprehension, reminders); 5-person cross-functional team; systematised decision logic validated with developers + clear documentation
- Outcome: "First livestreaming was a success" — 32% of user base joined; 13% submitted questions; 74% used hearts; 85% badge comprehension
- Research surfaced demand for native calendar integration + more product info for upcoming events

### 3. Merchants Onboarding (JP Morgan UK)
- Title: "Complex onboarding process to activate payments and enable payouts (web, mobile)" — KYB onboarding using JPM's SALT design system
- Role: Senior Product Designer (with product lead, PM, full-stack devs, legal, QA) · Timeline: Q3 2025
- Problem: merchants invited to additional programs got Sign Up links instead of Sign In; no automatic Sign In for ongoing onboardings → redundant steps and delays
- Solution: invitation-link logic detects existing accounts and serves the right CTA; automatic Sign In flows for active onboardings; streamlined marketplace selection after auth; edit multiple applications concurrently under one email
- Outcome: not stated on source site

### 4. Hackathon | APEX AI Assistant (🏆 6th place / 32 groups)
- Role: UX/UI Designer and Researcher · Timeline: 48 hours, June 2024
- Problem: 90% of JPM respondents faced tech problems preventing work; 67% had issues lasting 2+ hours; 34% found resolution hard/very hard; existing chatbot needed website access + agent support
- Process: research with 18 internal employees; 3 core user stories (issue identification, team communication, quick resolution); team of product designers, service designers, LLM architects, developers
- Solution: automated issue identification; learns from internal data + past queries; resolves via automated fixes, suggested solutions, or ticket escalation; works online and offline
